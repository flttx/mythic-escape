Design a completely NEW Kraken for a photoreal cinematic ocean survival game. This is a fresh creature design, not a variation of an earlier image. Produce one stunning wide landscape film frame, grounded live-action VFX realism, terrifying ancient sea animal, profound sense of immense weight and scale. Scene viewed from sea level close behind a tiny seven-metre wooden boat in the lower left foreground. A believable colossal cephalopod rises several hundred metres away in a storm-dark ocean, its 160-metre-high body dwarfs distant sea cliffs and lighthouse; its eight arms extend hundreds of metres outward and largely stay in the water. Build an elegant coherent ANATOMY first: a muscular compact head with two deep-set bronze eyes on opposite LATERAL sides, a broad thick smoothly vaulted mantle extending BACKWARD from the head at a shallow upward angle, with a rounded heavy crest rather than a spike. The mantle is robust but proportionate, approximately as tall as it is wide when viewed from the side; it is not a giant balloon. The head and mantle occupy only about ONE THIRD of the total visible creature silhouette height, the powerful reaching arms create the rest. A broad muscular circumoral arm crown below the eyes, separated roots merging with substantial organic tissue. No elephant-like trunk descending between front-facing human eyes: the two front arms emerge separately and splay to the left and right, leaving the head readable. EIGHT extremely long muscular arms with thick load-bearing bases, tapering continuously into fine ends, the visible long arcs imply slow heavy movement. One nearer arm rises like a forty-metre-thick living sea wall on the right, receding to the body in the middle distance; two far arms lift in large sweeping arcs, the remaining arms displace the ocean at the surface, with no ornamental tiny curls and no chaotic arm pile. Correct inner suction cups in two staggered rows, huge near the thick roots and gradually smaller toward tips. Near arm cups the size of doorways supply scale, not spikes or claws. Rich living abyssal skin: dark petrol blue and muted wine-brown chromatophores, stretched muscular skin with sparse deep tension folds and subtle fine pebbled pores, wet supple flesh, not stone, not bark, not cracked mud, no armored plates. Small subtle old scars, darker dorsal side, desaturated grey-brown ventral flesh. Strong anatomical landmarks read through atmosphere, natural asymmetry in pose. Immense water displacement, heavy sheets of water streaming off the arms, foaming radiating surge, rain and low sea fog. Layers of spray and clouds IN FRONT of the distant body make the distance and height legible. The near arm is sharply detailed, the body has atmospheric depth, far arms softened by rain. The seven-metre boat and distant lighthouse are minuscule beside the creature; physically credible lens and perspective, 28mm cinematic view, horizon low. Cold overcast dawn, soft pale light from upper left across wet folds and surf, restrained cinematic contrast with visible midtones. No humanoid face, no exaggerated angry eyebrows, no tentacles over the eyes, no pointed tall squid tower, no oversized egg-shaped balloon head, no pancake head, no short stumpy limbs, no flying weightless octopus, no cute sea animal, no coral or rocks growing on skin, no fantasy armor, no combat. A single powerful legible silhouette with real marine biology taken to mythic scale, like a new animal discovered in the deep ocean. No text, no UI, no diagrams, no collage. The creature should feel frighteningly plausible, cinematic and naturally proportioned, and the player absolutely tiny. Prioritize anatomical elegance and colossal presence over excessive surface noise.
